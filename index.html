Color doesn't exist.

Light has a wavelength. That's all it has.

  • Visible light is a sliver, about 380 to 700 nanometres, between infrared and ultraviolet.

Your eye writes down three numbers.

(every screen in this room is lying to you)

  • Three kinds of cone squash a whole spectrum into three numbers.
  • Different spectra with the same three numbers look identical. That's how RGB screens work.
  • First to go if the yes path runs long.
